Your league is all over this World Cup.

Here is every MLS name in every match you watch, so the two of you can do the thing you love most: point at the screen and say "he's ours."

Two on day one. Then Son.

PM
Mexico v South Africa · 2 MLS players, both in green and gold. South Africa's back line is part Eastern Conference: Olwethu Makhanya (Philadelphia Union, D) and Mbekezeli Mbokazi (Chicago Fire, D). The bragging rights

Numbers for the group chat.

⭐

LAFC leads the league

4 call-ups, the most of any MLS club: Son Heung-Min plus Canada's Eustáquio, Choinière, and Shaffelburg. Three of the four play within 24 hours of each other Thursday and Friday, all on your screens.

🍁

Canada is the MLS team

9 MLS players, the most of any nation at the tournament. Canada and the USA together account for nearly 40 percent of all MLS call-ups.

🇭🇹

Haiti's 52-year wait

Back at the World Cup for the first time since 1974, with three MLS players in the squad. Saturday 6 PM is the romance slot of your weekend.

🧤

A very MLS goalkeeper room

All three USA goalkeepers are MLS: Turner (New England), Freese (NYCFC), Brady (Chicago Fire). Whoever starts Friday, your league is in goal.

🌵

San Diego FC footnote

The expansion club sent 2 players (Cape Verde and Panama, both outside your weekend slate). The one who didn't go: Chucky Lozano, left off Mexico's longlist entirely.

🦅

Almirón's full circle

Paraguay's Miguel Almirón became a superstar at Atlanta United, left for Newcastle, and came back. Friday he walks into SoFi as the opponent. Respect pour, then beat him.
