LA Pride · 11 AM step-off

The parade takes the boulevard.

LA Pride's parade steps off at 11 AM on Hollywood Blvd (Highland to Cahuenga) with 100,000+ expected, and Pride Village runs 12:30 to 8:30 PM. After yesterday's drag brunch, this is the city throwing the afterparty.

Heads up · the FIFA pop-up sits near the parade

The game launch at 6021 Hollywood Blvd is blocks from the parade route on the same street. Expect closures, crowds, and slow rideshares in Hollywood from late morning on. Go at 10 AM doors before the parade peaks, or lean in: parade first, pop-up when it thins.
