The birthday anchor · Brunch with the Brunchettes

Drag brunch at Hamburger Mary's.

Mason and his twin Makenna share today's birthday, and the celebration is at Hamburger Mary's, 8288 Santa Monica Blvd in WeHo, the chain's oldest operating location (the original opened in San Francisco in 1972, by hippies and gay men). Weekend drag brunch seats at 11 AM, 1 PM, and 3 PM, doors about 30 minutes before each. Bottomless mimosas are $40 in the signature 20-ounce leg glass, VIP seating +$5, $7 per person show fee. Mychal will be there. Show up with hugs.
