The 26, and the armband twist.

Mauricio Pochettino named his 26-player roster on May 26. The headline: the captain is Tim Ream, the 38-year-old Charlotte FC center back. Christian Pulisic, who has worn the armband 29 times, was passed over. Pochettino backed Ream's experience for the role. That choice will be talked about on every broadcast; now you can talk about it first.

Paraguay · verified

Sixteen years of waiting.

Paraguay hasn't played a World Cup since 2010. Gustavo Alfaro, the Argentine who took Ecuador to Qatar 2022, qualified them sixth in CONMEBOL with 28 points, taking the final automatic berth. Over the last eight qualifying rounds they collected more points than any South American side, Argentina included. They will arrive at SoFi with nothing to apologize for.

Julio Enciso · the spark

Strasbourg attacker, 3 goals in qualifying. The one player who turns a Paraguay counter into a highlight. If he gets the ball running at the US back line, hold your breath.

🎩

Miguel Almirón · the familiar face

Back at Atlanta United after his Newcastle years. American crowds know him; he knows them right back. Craft and set-piece danger.

🧱

The backbone

Gustavo Gómez (Palmeiras) and Omar Alderete (Sunderland) anchor the defense, with Balbuena and Alonso behind them in the squad. This is the unit that posted those 10 clean sheets.

⚙️

Engine room + front

Cubas (Vancouver), Diego Gómez (Brighton), Bobadilla (São Paulo), Kaku Romero (Al Ain) in midfield; Sanabria (Cremonese), Sosa (Palmeiras), Pitta (Bragantino) up top.

Their shape · as scouted by credible outlets

A disciplined 4-4-2 that morphs into a 4-2-3-1, built on defensive solidity and quick transitions. Asunción was a fortress in qualifying. The blueprint against the US writes itself: sit low, stay narrow, release Enciso.

How Friday actually unfolds.

Everything above is sourced. This section is interpretation, labeled as such, so Quinn can argue with it at the AO party.

🔑

The matchup that decides it

US fullbacks against Paraguay's low block. Antonee Robinson's overlaps are the likeliest crowbar; Paraguay conceding 10 goals in 18 qualifiers says the crowbar will need patience. Expect long stretches of US possession that look like nothing is happening. That's the match working as designed.

⚠️

The danger pattern

US turnover in midfield, Enciso running at a retreating line, Almirón arriving late. If Paraguay scores first, the SoFi crowd becomes the most important player on the field, and Quinn becomes part of the starting XI.

🎯

Set pieces, both ways

Gustavo Gómez attacks corners; the US has the height to answer. Tournament openers are decided by set pieces more often than anyone admits. Watch the first US corner for the rehearsed routine.

📣

What to yell, and when

Pulisic gets the loudest welcome of the night, captain or not. Save a roar for Ream's first clearance; the armband story deserves a verdict from the stands. And if Zendejas comes on, the Club América fans in the building will tell you exactly how good he is.

The road after Friday

Group D continues: USA v Australia, June 19, Lumen Field, Seattle. Then USA v Türkiye, June 25, back at SoFi, where Gordon will be sitting next to Pete Hines. Saturday's 9 PM Australia v Türkiye match is your scouting reel; pour the Bosphorus Old Fashioned from the Watch + Sip page and take notes.
