The Match Bar · house inventions by Darby

Two palettes. Twelve matches.

Every Quinn pour is a Bulleit Old Fashioned variant. Every Gordon pour is Tito's, soda, and a splash. The theme changes; the palate never has to.

🥃

The House Pours

Quinn: Bulleit Old Fashioned, classic build, orange peel. Gordon: "The Bellamy" · Tito's, soda, splash of whichever juice the day deserves. When in doubt, order these.

🇲🇽

Thu · Mexico v South Africa

Quinn, "Azteca Old Fashioned": Bulleit, piloncillo or demerara syrup, chocolate-mole bitters, orange peel. Gordon, "Cielito Spritz": Tito's, soda, pineapple splash, hard lime squeeze, Tajín rim if you're brave. Toast the hosts' flavors. Wear your own colors.

🇺🇸

Fri · USA v Paraguay

Quinn, "The Outlaw": Bulleit, double orange, Luxardo cherry. He's at SoFi, so it's the victory pour when he lands back home. Gordon at Gym Bar, "Stars and Stripes Splash": Tito's, soda, cranberry + lemonade splashes, blueberry skewer. Red, white, blue, done.

🇧🇷

Sat · Brazil v Morocco

Gordon, "Copacabana Crush": Tito's, soda, pineapple, big lime squeeze. Caipirinha energy, Bellamy build. Quinn, "Maracanã Old Fashioned": Bulleit, demerara, lime twist instead of orange.

🔭

Sat late · Australia v Türkiye

The Group D scouting nightcap. Quinn, "Bosphorus Old Fashioned": Bulleit, sour-cherry syrup, orange peel. Gordon, "Harbour Splash": Tito's, soda, pineapple + lemonade. Take notes for June 25.

🥞

Sun · sendoff double

Germany v Curaçao, then Netherlands v Japan. Gordon, "Willemstad Sunrise": Tito's, soda, pineapple, slow cranberry float. Quinn, "Oranje Fashioned": Bulleit with a bar spoon of orange marmalade, bitters. One more round before the drive south.
